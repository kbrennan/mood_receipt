The seed is only the time: the current second goes through a hash into randomSeed() and noiseSeed(). Your mood picks the drawing technique, and the clock decides everything else. Every pixel is pure black or white, sized to a real thermal printer (384 dots for 58 mm paper, 576 for 80 mm). To save it, copy it or right-click / long-press the receipt.
